UKgent

UKgent is a small personal automation tool that helps its owner run their own YouTube Shorts channel. It writes short educational scripts, builds the videos, uploads them to the owner's own channel as private drafts for review, and gathers the channel's own statistics and comments so replies can be drafted.

Who it is for

UKgent is not a public service. It has one user: the person who runs it, signing in with their own Google account to manage their own YouTube channel. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups or services to anyone else.

What it does with Google access

With the owner's permission, it can upload videos to the owner's channel, read the owner's video statistics, and read and reply to comments on the owner's videos. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
